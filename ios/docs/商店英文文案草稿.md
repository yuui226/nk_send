## Description

ZTransfer is a photo transfer and shooting companion for compatible cameras. Connect your camera to browse photos, transfer files, view a live feed and apply photo effects.

BROWSE AND TRANSFER

Browse camera photos, add selected files to a transfer queue, follow progress and retry failed transfers. USB and compatible Wi-Fi connections are supported. Available features depend on the camera model, firmware and connection.

LIVE VIEW AND CAMERA CONTROL

View a live feed from compatible cameras and use supported shooting controls and monitoring tools. Portrait and landscape layouts accommodate different shooting setups. Controls vary with the camera and its current shooting mode.

PHOTO EFFECTS

Import photos and create results using filters, LUTs, borders and watermarks. Camera transfer effects and the local photo workbench keep separate settings. Some watermark options require Pro.

GPS LOCATION

With compatible equipment, send your phone's location to the camera for recording shooting locations. Bluetooth and location permission are required for the GPS connection. Background updates require the appropriate location permission. Place-name lookups may require an internet connection.

FREE USE AND PRO

The free version includes 25 successful original-file transfers per day, a 400 MiB limit per file and 180 seconds of actual live-view use per day. Pro removes these software quotas and unlocks the advanced watermark options listed on the purchase screen. Hardware and camera limitations still apply.

Pro is available as an annual auto-renewable subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. The annual subscription renews automatically unless canceled. Apple displays the applicable price and currency before purchase. Manage or cancel renewal in your Apple subscription settings. Buying lifetime Pro does not automatically cancel an existing annual subscription. A Restore Purchases option is available in the app.

BEFORE YOU START

Supports Nikon Z-series and selected D-series cameras. Features vary by model, firmware and connection. See https://ztransfer.top/ios/support/ for connection requirements.

Requires iOS 16.0 or later, or iPadOS 16.0 or later. For USB connections, use a compatible data cable; a USB 3.2 or higher specification cable is recommended. Actual speed depends on the phone, camera and connection.

Camera browsing, transfers and live view require compatible camera hardware. The iOS app does not record the live-view feed as a video on your phone. Recording control on the camera itself is available only on supported cameras and in supported modes.

ZTransfer is an independently developed tool and is not an official camera-manufacturer app. Camera brands and product names identify compatibility; their trademarks belong to their respective owners.
