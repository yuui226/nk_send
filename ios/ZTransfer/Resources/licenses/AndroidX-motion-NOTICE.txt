ZTransferAndroidMotion.swift and ZTransferAndroidMath.swift adapt the Float
tween, underdamped spring, duration estimation, cubic root solver and fast
cube root to native Swift. ZTransferScalarAnimation.swift follows Animatable's
first-frame, interruption and completion semantics. No Android/Kotlin runtime
is bundled or executed in the iOS product.

Sources: FloatAnimationSpec.kt, SpringSimulation.kt, SpringEstimation.kt,
Easing.kt, Animatable.kt, SuspendAnimation.kt, AnimateAsState.kt, Bezier.kt,
and ui-util/MathHelpers.kt from:
