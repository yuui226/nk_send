ZTransferAndroidColor.swift adapts the sRGB/Oklab conversion, Color packing,
half-precision rounding and fast cube-root routines to native Swift. It uses
resolved 1.7.6 transform matrix bit patterns and preserves Float32 arithmetic.
It does not bundle or execute the Android/Kotlin libraries in the iOS app.

Sources: Color.kt, Float16.kt, colorspace/Oklab.kt, colorspace/Rgb.kt,
colorspace/Connector.kt, colorspace/ColorSpace.kt, colorspace/ColorSpaces.kt,
and ui-util/MathHelpers.kt from:
https://dl.google.com/dl/android/maven2/androidx/compose/ui/ui-graphics/1.7.6/ui-graphics-1.7.6-sources.jar
https://dl.google.com/dl/android/maven2/androidx/compose/ui/ui-util/1.7.6/ui-util-1.7.6-sources.jar
