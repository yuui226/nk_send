ZTransfer 隐私政策

生效及最近更新：2026 年 7 月 28 日

ZTransfer 是一款让 Android 设备连接尼康相机、浏览、传输照片并进行遥控拍摄的工具。本政策说明应用处理哪些信息以及为什么处理。

我们处理的信息

  • 相机与本地文件：照片、视频、实时取景画面和用户选择的保存目录只在设备与相机之间处理。我们不会把这些内容上传到 ZTransfer 的服务器。
  • 授权信息：激活、恢复购买或续签时，应用会向授权服务器发送不可逆散列后的设备标识、设备型号、应用版本、授权码或购买凭据，用于签发和维护高级版权益。
  • Google Play 购买：Play 版购买时，Google Play 处理付款资料。应用会把商品 ID 和不透明的 purchase token 发给 ZTransfer 服务器，并由服务器向 Google 验证购买、处理恢复、续订和退款。我们不接收银行卡号或完整付款资料。
  • 网络与安全信息：服务器会处理 IP 地址和请求时间，用于限流、防滥用、故障排查和安全审计。
  • 可选的麦克风数据：只有用户授权并主动使用带声音的取景器录像时才在本机录制；拒绝权限仍可进行无声录像。

权限用途

网络和 Wi-Fi 权限用于连接相机及授权服务；前台服务、通知和唤醒锁用于保持用户发起的传输；USB Host 用于连接受支持的相机；麦克风权限仅用于可选的录像音轨。Play 版不请求安装未知应用权限，更新由 Google Play 提供。

共享、保留与安全

我们不出售个人数据，不投放基于个人数据的广告，也不内置第三方行为分析 SDK。数据只会在提供上述功能所必需时与 Google Play、服务器托管和网络服务提供方处理，或在法律要求时披露。授权与交易记录会在提供权益、处理退款/争议、防欺诈及满足法律义务所需的期限内保留；不再需要时会删除或匿名化。我们使用加密传输、签名授权凭据和访问控制降低风险，但互联网系统无法保证绝对安全。

用户选择与删除请求

用户可以拒绝可选权限、停止使用并卸载应用。需要查询、更正或删除与授权有关的数据时，可通过 Google Play 商品页“开发者联系方式”中的电子邮件联系我们；国内用户也可通过 QQ 953000922 联系。为避免泄露，我们可能需要先核验相关授权或购买凭据。

儿童与政策变更

ZTransfer 面向相机用户，并非专为儿童设计。政策发生重要变化时，我们会更新本页的日期，并在适当情况下通过应用或商品页提示。

ZTransfer Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: July 28, 2026

ZTransfer connects an Android device to a supported Nikon camera to browse and transfer media and to control the camera. This policy explains what information the app processes and why.

Information we process

  • Camera and local files: Photos, videos, live-view frames, and the selected save folder are processed between your camera and device. ZTransfer does not upload this content to its servers.
  • License information: During activation, purchase restoration, or renewal, the app sends a one-way hashed device identifier, device model, app version, and the relevant license or purchase credential to the license server so it can issue and maintain Pro entitlements.
  • Google Play purchases: Google Play processes payment details. The Play build sends the product ID and an opaque purchase token to the ZTransfer server, which verifies it with Google and handles restoration, renewal, and refunds. We do not receive card numbers or full payment details.
  • Network and security information: The server processes IP addresses and request times for rate limiting, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and security auditing.
  • Optional microphone data: Audio is recorded locally only after permission is granted and you start a live-view recording with sound. If permission is denied, silent recording remains available.

Permissions

Network and Wi-Fi permissions connect to the camera and licensing services. Foreground-service, notification, and wake-lock permissions keep a transfer you started running. USB Host connects supported cameras. Microphone access is only for the optional recording audio track. The Play build does not request permission to install unknown apps; Google Play provides its updates.

Sharing, retention, and security

We do not sell personal data, serve personalized advertising, or include third-party behavioral analytics SDKs. Data is processed by Google Play and our hosting or network providers only as needed for the purposes above, or disclosed when required by law. License and transaction records are retained as needed to provide entitlements, handle refunds or disputes, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations; they are deleted or anonymized when no longer needed. We use encrypted transport, signed license credentials, and access controls, but no Internet system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and deletion requests

You may deny optional permissions, stop using the app, and uninstall it. To request access, correction, or deletion of licensing data, contact us using the email shown under “Developer contact” on the ZTransfer Google Play listing. Users in China may also contact QQ 953000922. We may need to verify the relevant license or purchase credential before acting on a request.

Children and changes

ZTransfer is intended for camera users and is not designed specifically for children. If this policy changes materially, we will update the date on this page and provide an additional notice in the app or store listing when appropriate.
